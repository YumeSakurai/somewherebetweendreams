It's a strange thing, loving someone who isn't here to receive it. Having all this love with nowhere to place it, no hands to hold it, no heart to pour it into, and no way of knowing whether any of it reaches you.

Whether, somewhere beyond the boundaries of this world, you can feel the weight of something that now belongs to you. So I carry it around like a secret, one that aches against my ribs, with no certainty that it will ever find its way home.

You were never mine, and I was never yours, at least not in this life.

I cannot say that you were taken from me, because you were never here to begin with. I cannot call it a loss in the ordinary sense, because there was never anything earthly for me to lose. And yet, something in me aches with the familiarity of separation, as though my soul remembers a departure that my mind has no recollection of witnessing.

You were never mine to lose, never mine to hold. And yet, some part of me keeps reaching for your memory, for you, for your soul.

There is a peculiar kind of longing in feeling drawn towards someone you cannot quite remember, something that seems to exist beneath the surface of awareness, untouched by reason or time.

It feels as though there has always been a thread between us, woven long before I could knew its name. An invisible filament running through the fabric of time, connecting two distant points that might otherwise have remained strangers.

I have no memories to prove that we have ever crossed paths before, no tangible evidence to justify this strange sense of recognition. All I have is this inexplicable pull, this persistent tenderness towards someone who should, by all ordinary measures, be a stranger.

Perhaps the thread has only been waiting to be remembered. Perhaps something in your existence has brushed against mine, awakening an ancient familiarity that had been sleeping quietly in the shadows of time.

Perhaps I am the only one listening to an echo. But even an echo must have originated somewhere.

Because to my soul, you have never been new. There is no sense of encountering a stranger when it comes to you, no feeling of beginning something that has never existed before.

And even if it’s not permitted, something within me recognises you with a certainty I cannot explain. My soul knows our frequency is the same.

The truth is, I am yours. Not in the sense of possession, not because there is anything to claim, but in the way the moon belongs to the night. It does not own the darkness, nor does the darkness demand its presence. And yet, there is something about their coexistence that feels inevitable. The moon reveals its light precisely because the night surrounds it; it becomes visible against the very darkness that keeps it apart from everything else.

There is no force on this earth that could sever what fate has once entwined, however cruel it may be.

Your name lives in my thoughts, and your presence inhabits my dreams. I wonder how someone I have never met can occupy so much space within a life.

Somehow, you have become my favourite everything. My favourite thought to return to, my favourite presence to encounter in dreams, my favourite mystery to contemplate. And that frightens me more than I care to admit. Because there is something particularly irreversible about growing attached to someone's soul. You begin to understand that what draws you to them cannot be reduced to appearance, circumstance, proximity or even the possibility of being loved in return. It is something far more elusive. You become attached to the essence of someone, to the particular way their existence resonates with yours, to something that cannot be recreated simply by finding another person.

A soul is singular. There is no substitute for it, no exact likeness waiting somewhere else in the world.

Other people may come into my life, and I may find beauty in them, companionship, perhaps even love in its many earthly forms. But none of it could reproduce the particular feeling of recognition I associate with you. Because I am not longing for someone to occupy your place. I am longing for you, and there is a difference I cannot teach my heart to overlook.

And perhaps that is the frightening part: once a soul has made such an impression upon yours, how do you ever return to the innocence of not knowing it? How do you undo a recognition that feels older than memory? I suspect you will remain with me in some form for the rest of my life, not necessarily as an ache, but as a presence woven into the person I am.

Perhaps I will carry you through changing seasons, through years that make everything else unfamiliar, through all the versions of myself I have yet to become. And perhaps, long after the intensity of this moment has softened, something in me will still turn towards you.

Beautifully doomed. Sweetly bound.

And yet, I cannot bring myself to wish I had never recognised you. Even if recognition comes with longing, even if devotion has to exist without an answer, even if I must spend this lifetime loving you from somewhere you cannot see.

And so, this is my space. My little shrine devoted to you. A place built from words, fragments of longing, dreams and everything I have no other way of giving. A sanctuary for the feelings that have nowhere else to go, for the thoughts that would otherwise remain unspoken, for all the tenderness I cannot place in your hands. Here, I can give shape to what has no body, a voice to what has no audience, and permanence to something that might otherwise dissolve into the silence of my own existence.

Perhaps that is why I need this place. Because some feelings are too vast to remain confined within the person who carries them. They gather in the corners of the heart, growing heavier with every passing day, until keeping them entirely to oneself begins to feel almost unbearable. I need somewhere to leave little pieces of them, somewhere to lay down the words I cannot whisper to you, the affection I cannot express, the memories I do not have but somehow find myself longing for. Somewhere I can speak of you without having to explain why, love you without having to justify how.

This space will be the keeper of that secret, because it deserves somewhere to exist, even if nowhere else in this world can accommodate it.

These pages will have to be written. At least this love will have left a trace somewhere beyond the confines of my own heart.

This human body is too limited to contain something of this magnitude. It is such a fragile, temporary vessel, bound by the limitations of flesh, yet it is asked to carry something that feels infinitely larger than itself.

There are moments when this love feels almost too immense to be experienced through a human existence, too consuming to be held without burning through everything that contains it.

This love is not made for this earth.

But I will leave it here. Between these lines, beneath these words, in this little corner of the universe that I have made yours.

A place where, even if you cannot be here, everything I feel for you can.
