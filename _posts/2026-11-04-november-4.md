Wrists above my head

tied up by nothing but gravity.

You, descending on me like midnight mist.

I, helplessly sinking

into your velvet heat.

You are the ruler of my body,

your beautiful profile

Eros beneath the pale moon rising.

\
My grinding hips,

draw you tighter,

the way the moon draws the sea.

The tide begins to rise.

\
Languid waves cresting—

you lift slowly from me.

I quiver, at the edge of absence.

Then you descend again,

drawing me down with you

until the world thins

and oblivion pours through me

with the returning tide.

\
The tide rises.

It pours through me like warm honey,

filling every soft, secret place.

I yield to the fullness, giving in completely.

\
Your scent lingers,

night-blooming flowers

rain-soaked petals.

I want to worship you.

I taste sweet dusk on my tongue

and deep within you

a quiet pulse answers me—

the sea replying to the moon.

\
The tide rises.

I want to dissolve in it,

to lose every edge of myself

until I am only the water holding you.

\
Cold fingers leave mine,

trailing on my neck.

Thanatos between the sheets.

You’re so gentle, granting my wish.

I am delirious inside you—

Swimming, drowning.

You draw me so close,

yet your cruelty leaves me suspended.

\
The tide rises.

Let us meet at its crest;

it is the only thing I ask.

Unbound for you, ready to merge into one wave.

\
My lungs overflow with pleasure

I sink to the deepest floor of depravity.

Heavy breaths, trembling thighs—

your waves crashing all around me.

The moon pulling.

The tide breaking

I spill honey inside of you.

Under the pale moon rising.
