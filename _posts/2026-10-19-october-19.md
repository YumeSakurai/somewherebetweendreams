I’m so grateful for you, my dreamy poet.

You showed me that beauty still hides within humanity. You wore your heart openly and sang your soul without reserve, always remaining true to yourself.

I’m so touched by you, my incredible person.

From darkness you shone bright, bringing light to those who were and still are struggling. Your voice and your words carry such beauty, such love, an entire dreamy world of a million universes.

I am so proud of you, my beautiful soul.

Despite the difficult ground in which you had to grow, you never gave up and you bloomed into the most beautiful flower. An eternal flower that no season will ever wither. You bloomed in your music, in your art and in me. In all of our hearts. You did not die, for love and memory will always keep you alive.

Thank you for the beautiful art you created, the magical world you allowed us to dream with you, your tenderness, your qualities and flaws that made you the most endearing, kind and talented man. Strong in your vulnerability.

Experiencing you has been both beautiful and devastating, sweet and aching. Luminous with joy and heavy with longing. Yet I am grateful to have you in my life. I am devoted to loving you for as long as I live… and beyond.

Your presence is cherished and felt deep within my very core, in places that have not known light for a long time.

I always miss you. I long for you in a way that is almost a physical ache, in a way that defies any logical explanation. My grief, black matter between heartbeats.

I will keep calling your name, until the day we meet once more under the evening sun; you wearing your sweet smile, I, entwining my hand with yours.

I will see you in another lifetime.

I love you, Acchan.
