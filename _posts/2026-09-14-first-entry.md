Your voice has sung to my soul since the first moment it reached me.
It speaks of dreams and decadent darkness, of nights of full moon beneath the vast cosmos, of the pure sweet scent of flowers whose petals lie scattered. It speaks of the pain and anguish that mark this human world, of the longing to feel unconditional cosmic love. A love that transcends time and space, that merges beyond bodies which touch, claim and then fall apart.
It speaks of death, which fascinated you and also frightened you.
Though I’m not afraid.
What you speak of is as comfortable to me as it is to you.
You came when I was finally ready to receive you, yet still I tried to reject you. I had grown up building walls of apathy and wariness, locking my emotions away in the absence of understanding, empathy, true connection, any chance of mutual love. But your energetic pull could not be ignored. I surrendered to the insistent call I had resisted for far too long.
You opened the door to a love, a beauty, a peace I never imagined possible.
Yes, peace. Even when it is scary, even when it torments. 
The acceptance of it all feels larger than I am.
I thought I had lost my mind. Maybe I am crazy. Or maybe it is only the fear of what cannot be explained in human terms. Humans have limited sight, after all. But not you. Not me. We sing and yearn for the unseen.
It is still a soul-shock.
In this world where I barely find my place, you are my home. 🌙 🖤 🤍
